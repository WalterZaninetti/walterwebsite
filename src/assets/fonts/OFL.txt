Only the Latin subset of each family is included. The files were taken from the
Google Fonts CDN, which distributes the same OFL-licensed originals.

Source Serif 4 is shipped as a variable font instanced down from what Google
serves: weight 400-700 rather than 200-900, and — for the italic — optical size
pinned at 20. That is a permitted modification under the OFL; the name is
unchanged because the glyphs are.
